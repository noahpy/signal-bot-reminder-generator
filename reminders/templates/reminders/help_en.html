How reminders work

1. What happens when you save?

The reminder is checked and sent to the bot. From then on, every day at the set time (German time, including daylight saving), the bot checks whether a message is due today and posts it into this page's Signal group. Each reminder sends at most one message per day.

2. Date vs. reminder day

When? defines the dates (e.g. "every Monday"). Remind days before only moves the message: with 4, the reminder arrives 4 days before the date; with 0, on the date itself.

3. Repeats

Weekly
On the ticked weekdays. "Every 2 weeks" needs a Start date to count from.
Monthly
On one day of the month. 31 means the last day of the month (February too). Optionally only in certain months (e.g. November–April only).
Every N days
Every N days from the Start date, e.g. 14 = every two weeks on the start date's weekday.
List of specific dates
One date per line, optionally followed by names, e.g. 23.11.2026 Lilli or 30.11.2026 Leon, Adi. If names are given, exactly those people are responsible. Without names, the people selected under Who? apply (with "Rotate": taking turns line by line). Names are matched against the Signal group's members (a unique first name is enough).
Once
Exactly one date.

Until (optional) ends the repetition: there are no dates after it.

4. Who gets mentioned?

  • Everyone selected every time: all ticked people are @mentioned on every date.
  • Rotate: one person per date, taking turns in the order of the Who? list (people already in the reminder are listed first in their rotation order; newly ticked people are added at the end). Counting starts at the Start date: the first date from Start is the first person, the second date the second person, and so on. So the Start date decides who goes first. Without a Start date the order is still fixed, but who goes first is not predictable. Check it in the preview.

The mention points to the Signal account, not the name: everyone sees the person as saved in their own Signal, and the person gets a notification. Someone who has left the group is only named in the text (no notification).

5. Message text

The text can contain these placeholders:

  • {mentions} – @mentions of the responsible people
  • {names} – their names as plain text (no notification)
  • {title} – the reminder's title
  • {date} – the date, e.g. 23.11.2026
  • {weekday} – the date's weekday in German, e.g. Montag
  • {days_until} – days until the date (0 = today)
  • {timing} – ready-made German text: „heute fällig“, „morgen fällig (24.11.)“ or „fällig am Montag, 23.11. (in 4 Tagen)“

Example: Hey {mentions}, eine Erinnerung für "{title}" – {timing} :)
becomes: Hey @Lilli, eine Erinnerung für "Mülldienst" – fällig am Montag, 23.11. (in 4 Tagen) :)

6. Trying it out

  • Preview lists every message of the next 120 days with date and text, without saving or sending anything.
  • Send now (in the overview) posts today's (or the next) reminder to the group immediately.
  • Pause stops a reminder without deleting it. Unticking Active does the same.
  • Log shows when the bot sent what.
